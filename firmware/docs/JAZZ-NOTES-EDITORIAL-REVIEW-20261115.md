# Jazz Notes — 20-case musical relevance review (2026-11-15)

**Scope:** unreleased local content, **not** a hardware/release acceptance.
This is an editorial spot-check of heterogeneous entries, not a scientific
validation of all 1,498 keys. Protected biographies, sourced research prose
and MusicBrainz-derived claims have **different evidence levels**. A title
obtained from a recording performer relationship is merely an example: it
must not silently turn into an artist's signature work.

**Important distinctions:** A source URL in an SD row is provenance, not proof
that all historical clauses were independently authenticated. Six borderline
people in this review now have deliberately *genre-specific* summaries rather
than a generic "jazz musician" label. A MusicBrainz relationship is still a
community-maintained credit; it cannot establish an artist's influence or
critical importance by itself. All 20 entries need only pass the content
schema and provenance checks; the **full** encyclopedia remains incompletely
reviewed for artistic significance.

## Regression conditions

1. Maintain 1,498 original keys; do not merge an ensemble into a similarly
   named solo artist. Original seed checksum must remain identical.
2. Keep 63 protected editor biographies and 36 hand-selected MB albums
   byte-for-byte; new researched capsules have unique keys and citations.
3. Suppress explicitly out-of-scope film/book P800 choices on the LCD without
   removing their original source statements from the PC-side JSON cache.
4. Treat identity conflicts, absent P434, failed HTTP and multi-venue events
   as **unproven**; never turn them into invented music credits.
5. The source review does **not** establish Android/SD/ESP32 hardware readiness.
